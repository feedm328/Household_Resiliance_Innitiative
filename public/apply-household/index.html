About the Fund

The Household Resilience Fund (HRF) provides direct financial assistance to qualifying households experiencing hardship, unexpected medical expenses, loss of income, or another emergency that threatens household stability.

The Fund is the flagship emergency assistance program of The Household Resilience Initiative. It was created to help individuals and families overcome an unexpected financial crisis before temporary hardship becomes long-term instability.

Assistance is reviewed individually, with dignity, respect, and compassion. Depending on verified need and available funding, assistance may be delivered directly to a landlord, utility provider, pharmacy, or other verified vendor.

How to Apply

Applications are accepted online. Complete the confidential multi-step form with accurate household, income, housing, and hardship information. Pre-approved applicants will be asked to provide verifying documentation before funding is confirmed.

Individuals and families may apply directly or be referred by a school, healthcare provider, faith-based organization, social worker, community organization, housing agency, government agency, or partner nonprofit.

Eligibility

The Household Resilience Fund is intended for individuals and families facing genuine financial hardship that affects their ability to maintain a safe and stable household. Receiving assistance from another program does not automatically disqualify an applicant.

Priority may be given to households experiencing:

  • Loss of employment or income
  • Medical emergency, serious illness, or disability
  • Housing instability or eviction risk
  • Utility shut-off notices
  • Domestic violence or safety concerns
  • Natural disaster or displacement
  • Dependent children
  • Seniors on fixed incomes
  • Veterans
  • Individuals with disabilities
  • Caregivers facing hardship
  • Other documented emergencies

Eligible Assistance

Assistance may be available for verified essential household expenses.

Housing & utilities

  • Past-due rent and mortgage support
  • Security deposits and emergency lodging
  • Electricity, gas, water, sewer, and heating fuel
  • Internet needed for work or education

Food & essentials

  • Groceries and fresh produce
  • Infant formula and diapers
  • Hygiene and cleaning supplies

Health & transport

  • Prescription medications
  • Medical equipment and transport
  • Emergency healthcare costs
  • Public transit, fuel, and essential repairs

Emergency needs

  • Clothing and household essentials
  • Disaster recovery supplies
  • Basic furniture when necessary

Child Household Support

Households with dependent children under 17 may be eligible for up to $450 in additional assistance per child. This support helps reduce the financial burden on parents during a crisis and may cover needs such as food, diapers, formula, clothing, school supplies, housing stability, or childcare.

Meeting eligibility criteria does not guarantee funding. Funding decisions are based on verified need, program guidelines, and resources available at the time of review.
